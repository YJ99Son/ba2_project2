Text Embedding

Amenity Embedding

Model: SentenceTransformer (all-MiniLM-L6-v2)

Raw amenity data contains 6,372 unique terms. Traditional one-hot encoding creates a sparse matrix, losing semantic connections.

We embedded these terms into 384-dimensional dense vectors, capturing semantic similarities (e.g., Wifi ≈ Internet).
